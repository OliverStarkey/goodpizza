# goodpizza
Do you like pizza gnarly or as a perfect circle?

#Y12 PERSONAL PROJECT 1#

Utilising Fourier Series to identify a pizza's shape to the most popular by general consensus in order to maximise enjoyability. 
My idea is to provide mock training data to AI in Python with U-Net in order to generate a ranking of sorts of the most popular pizza shape equations, and leverage that to compare its likeness to the photographed pizza's shape, thereby identifying its percentage likelihood of maximal enjoyment.
